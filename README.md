Entry for the make.fun competition
[The Dog Reviews the Mailman](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-the-dog-reviews-the-mailman-87608f85).

`main.py` writes one funny one-line review a dog gives the mailman
(121 characters, one line) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Five stars. He comes every day, lets me bark at him, and never once asked me to stop. The only human who respects my job.

Run: `python3 main.py` — output is deterministic (byte-identical every run).
